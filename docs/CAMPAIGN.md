# Campaign plan

This is a proposed structure, not a set of playable chapters. The titles, region partitions and mechanical emphases are authored. The broad chronology is based on the scholarly biography registered as `treccani-garibaldi-1999`; dates must be researched at mission level before historical scenes are shipped.

| Chapter | Planning interval | Regional focus | Development purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A life at sea | 1824–1832 | Nice/Nizza and Mediterranean routes | Learn movement, work, travel and trust |
| The road into exile | 1833–1835 | Ligurian ports and Marseille | Establish contacts, information and departure |
| The other world | 1836–1848 | Southern Brazil and Montevideo | Develop maritime operations and volunteer forces |
| Return and retreat | 1848–1849 | Northern Italy, Rome and a retreat corridor | Link defence, command and withdrawal |
| Between campaigns | 1850–1854 | New York and sea routes | Provide an authored interlude and continuity |
| The volunteer army | 1859–1860 | Alps, Sicily and southern Italy | Test expedition logistics and political limits |
| Victory is not sovereignty | 1862–1871 | Later Italian campaigns and France | Develop delegation and changing authority |
| The man and the legend | 1872–1882 | Caprera | A proposed reflective epilogue |

The intervals deliberately overlap at transitions and leave some years outside playable scope. They are not a complete day-by-day timeline. All regions beyond the harbour are manifest entries only.

## First authored scenario

`nizza_harbour_1824` is a work errand, not a retelling of a named historical incident. Garibaldi is the player identity; the dock contact and captain are unnamed fictional people, and the label `storehouse` identifies an interaction point, not a historical building. No precise day, ship identity, family meeting or political affiliation is asserted by the scene.

## Research queue

Develop claim-level records for Anita, Giuseppe Mazzini, relevant local commanders, volunteers, political interlocutors and opponents before implementing their interactions. Use a stable person ID and dated relationships. Do not spawn later associates in the opening chapter to make a tutorial more convenient.

For each future scene, decide what is documented, what is inferred, what is compressed and what is invented. Track disagreements instead of replacing them with a convenient dramatic version. Retellings, memoirs and public imagery need author and date context; the title's heroic frame is not a universal NPC viewpoint.

## Region transitions

A future transition must declare departure and arrival regions, elapsed historical time, surviving people, equipment, supply changes and which contacts can still communicate. Historical death or political events need explicit policy and evidence. None of these transitions executes in this scaffold.
