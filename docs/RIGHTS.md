# Rights and asset policy

The owner-declared group hierarchy has **Notation Systems Inc.** as parent and **Notations Gaming**, **Notation Manufacturing** and **Notations Laboratories** as current child-company names; see [Organization](../README.md#organization). **Notations Gaming** is the current identity for game development. Recorded licensing names and copyright identities retain their existing attribution.

The original scaffold did not select or alter project licensing. This separate, explicitly requested licensing adoption identifies **Notation Systems Inc.** as the owner/licensor of original project material based on the [owner-supplied declaration](OWNERSHIP_DECLARATION.md). The existing rights notice records **Cartesian Graphics Ltd.** and **Giason Pooni Studios** as earlier issuing names/labels, not automatically additional owners. [LICENSE](../LICENSE) is the applicable permission-only rights reservation for material actually owned by Notation Systems Inc.

Uses requiring permission need prior outreach and an express written grant identifying the legal licensor holding the relevant rights, signed by its authorized representative. Group affiliation alone does not establish asset ownership, incorporation or agency. Submit a [licensing request](https://github.com/atomtrapping/A-Man-of-Two-Worlds/issues/new?template=licensing-request.yml); a request, payment, attribution or public visibility does not create permission. Public-platform permissions, prior valid grants, mandatory exceptions and third-party terms remain effective. This notice does not create an executed licence, contract by browsing, automatic penalty, or worldwide legal certification.

Do not add MIT, GPL, a commercial EULA or another copyright-holder name without the owner's explicit decision for this project. Preserve licensing work conducted separately from ordinary game development.

No code from 1792 is incorporated. Its use as a design reference does not change either project's terms. No commercial-game assets, historical scans or portrait images are included.

Before adding third-party content, record its creator, source URL, exact licence or permission, modification notes and required attribution. Before distributing an engine build, include the applicable engine and third-party notices. Code, writing, music, fonts, models and historical images may have different terms; maintain that separation.

Asset provenance belongs next to the asset or in an auditable manifest. Do not claim that an online image is reusable solely because its subject lived in the nineteenth century.

## Reference corpus and original expression

The owner has stated that project assets are original and the corpus is used only for reference. A reference source is evidence for research, not a licence to copy its protected expression or a proprietary asset of this project. Citations, external links, archive metadata and historical facts do not transfer rights in the underlying works. Independently authored fictional writing, scene arrangements and reconstructions remain distinguishable from the sources that inform them.

The current [baseline audit](LICENSING_AUDIT.md) identifies two source-register URLs, original primitive-based scenes, and no imported media. Future original assets require creator and title/provenance records; any external inclusion requires a separate rights review. The notice preserves existing valid permissions and does not retroactively withdraw rights granted elsewhere.

AI/model training, automated extraction and text and data mining require separate express authorization to the extent the relevant rights may lawfully be reserved. Required machine-readable reservations must be implemented where the material is published or delivered; prose alone is not a certification of operational compliance.
