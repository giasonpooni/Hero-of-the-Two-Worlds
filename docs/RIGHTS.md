# Rights and asset policy

This scaffolding change does not select or alter project licensing. Repository-level licensing documents, when present, govern the applicable terms. Do not infer a grant from public visibility. Do not add MIT, GPL, a commercial EULA or a copyright-holder name without the owner's explicit decision for this project. Preserve licensing work conducted separately from the game scaffold.

No code from 1792 is incorporated. Its use as a design reference does not change either project's terms. No commercial-game assets, historical scans or portrait images are included.

Before adding third-party content, record its creator, source URL, exact licence or permission, modification notes and required attribution. Before distributing an engine build, include the applicable engine and third-party notices. Code, writing, music, fonts, models and historical images may have different terms; maintain that separation.

Asset provenance belongs next to the asset or in an auditable manifest. Do not claim that an online image is reusable solely because its subject lived in the nineteenth century.
